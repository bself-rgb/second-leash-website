Pet Help & Crisis Resources

Need help keeping your dog safe during a difficult time? Start here.

When life gets hard, pet care can become hard too. This guide helps Arkansas pet owners find the right kind of support during incarceration, treatment, hospitalization, housing instability, domestic violence, financial hardship, or another temporary crisis.

For the full list of Arkansas organizations, use the Second Leash Community Directory. This page is designed to help you figure out where to start.

Start With Arkansas 211

Arkansas 211 is a free information and referral service that can connect Arkansas residents with local help for food, housing, transportation, health care, treatment, domestic violence services, and other needs.

Visit Arkansas 211 Call 211 or text your ZIP code to 898211.

Know a Resource We Should Add?

We're continuing to build the directory. If you know an Arkansas organization that provides meaningful help to pet owners or families, send it to Second Leash. Please include the organization's name, service, location, website, and best contact information.

Important: Resources, eligibility, hours, geographic coverage, fees, and availability can change. Always confirm current information directly with the organization before relying on a resource. Second Leash does not endorse every organization listed in the Community Directory.
