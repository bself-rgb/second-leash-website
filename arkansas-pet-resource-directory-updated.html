Arkansas Pet Resource Directory

A growing directory of pet resources for Arkansas families, foster caregivers, referral partners, and people facing a temporary crisis.

Looking for help with your pet? Start with the category that best matches your situation. We are building this directory to make Arkansas pet resources easier to find in one place.

Important: Resources, eligibility, hours, geographic coverage, fees, and availability can change. Always confirm current information directly with the organization before relying on a resource. Second Leash does not endorse every organization listed here.

Start Here: Statewide Directories

Arkansas 211

Free information and referral service for Arkansas residents. Search or ask about food, housing, transportation, domestic violence services, treatment, health care, and other local assistance.

Pet Food & Basic Needs

CARE Pet Food Pantry — Little Rock

Central Arkansas Rescue Effort for Animals operates a pet food pantry at its Little Rock location. The current policy says the pantry is open to Arkansas residents who can arrange pickup; availability depends on food supplies.

5516 Kavanaugh Blvd., Little Rock, AR 72207
(501) 603-2273

Low-Cost Veterinary & Spay/Neuter Resources

Arkansans for Animals — Operation S.A.V.E.

Little Rock-area nonprofit clinic focused on low-cost spay/neuter and basic pet wellness services. Confirm current services, eligibility, and pricing before scheduling.

Visit Arkansans for Animals

Companions Spay & Neuter Clinic

Low-cost spay/neuter provider in the Greenbrier area. The clinic focuses on sterilization services and related preventive care; appointments and eligibility requirements may apply.

Visit Companions Spay & Neuter
