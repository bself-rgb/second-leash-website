Contact Second Leash

Reach out about temporary foster care, volunteering, donations, referrals, or partnerships.

Contact information

  • Phone: (870) 456-3950
  • Email: bself@secondleash.us
  • Based in: Searcy, Arkansas
  • Service area: Arkansas statewide and nationwide when possible
  • Availability: Anytime
  • Preferred contact: Text or email due to limited cellular service at home
Response expectations: Messages are reviewed as quickly as possible, but Second Leash is not a staffed emergency hotline. Contact does not guarantee placement.

What to include

  • Your name and safe contact method
  • Your city and state
  • The reason temporary care is needed
  • The date care would need to begin
  • Estimated length of care
  • Dog’s age, size, breed or mix, sex, health, and behavior
  • Transportation needs
  • Whether you are the legal owner or an authorized referral partner
