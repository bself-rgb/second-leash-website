Donate

Your support helps dogs stay safe while their people work toward stability.

Give with purpose

Help keep a family from being separated forever

Donations help Second Leash provide food, veterinary care, prevention medicine, crates, bedding, cleaning supplies, transportation, and reunification support for dogs in temporary foster care.

Second Leash is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt nonprofit organization. EIN: 33-3031631. Donations may be tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

100% of every donation goes to the direct care of the dogs in our program.

How donations may be used

  • Food and essential supplies
  • Approved veterinary examinations and treatment
  • Vaccines and prevention medicine
  • Crates, gates, bedding, and safety equipment
  • Transportation and reunification costs
  • Program administration necessary to provide services

Donation transparency

Unless a donation is accepted with a specific written restriction, Second Leash may use funds where they are most needed to advance its charitable mission. Restricted gifts are used for the stated purpose when accepted; excess or unusable restricted funds will be handled according to applicable law and donor communications.
