Why This Matters to Second Leash

Help Us Build a Wall of Remembrance

Behind every statistic was a person: someone's child, parent, sibling, partner, friend, neighbor, coworker, or loved one. A person with a laugh, favorite song, inside jokes, dreams, quirks, and a story worth remembering.

Throughout September, we invite you to submit a photo and a short tribute honoring someone whose life was lost to suicide. Tell us who they were, what you loved about them, what you miss, or what you wish the world knew about them.

September 10 • World Suicide Prevention Day

On September 10, we will pause the giveaways to center remembrance, prevention, connection, and hope. We will honor those we have lost, recognize those who are still fighting, and remind our community that asking for help is a sign that your story is still being written.

You matter. Your story matters. Stay.

If You Need Help Right Now

If you are in the United States and are experiencing suicidal thoughts, emotional distress, or a mental health crisis, the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is available by phone, text, or online chat.

Call 988 Text 988 Chat with 988

Second Leash is not a crisis counseling service. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
