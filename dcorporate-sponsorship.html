Why partner with us

Help remove a barrier between people and the help they need

Fear of losing a beloved dog can keep someone from entering treatment, accepting hospitalization, complying with a court requirement, or leaving an unsafe situation. Second Leash creates a temporary bridge: the dog is cared for, ownership remains intact, and reunification stays at the center of the plan.

Corporate partners help us respond faster, support foster homes, and protect more families from unnecessary permanent separation.

“No one should have to choose between getting help and keeping their dog.”
