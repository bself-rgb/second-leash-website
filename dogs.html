Why stories are shared carefully

Stories help supporters understand the value of temporary foster care, but the people we serve deserve dignity and privacy. Second Leash avoids sensationalizing crisis, disclosing unnecessary personal information, or using a person’s circumstances as a fundraising spectacle.
