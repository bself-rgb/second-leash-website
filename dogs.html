Temporary care. Lasting love.

Our foster families offer a safe place for dogs while their people navigate a difficult chapter. These stories are shared with care, without unnecessary details about the people we serve.

Behind every pup is a story. Behind every story is hope.

A temporary crisis shouldn't have to mean a permanent goodbye. Whether someone is seeking safety, treatment, medical care, or stable housing, Second Leash works to preserve the human–animal bond through compassionate temporary foster care.

We protect the privacy and dignity of the people we serve, and we share their circumstances only with appropriate permission.
