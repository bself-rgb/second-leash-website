Frequently asked questions

Does submitting a form guarantee placement?

No. Placement depends on safety, foster availability, timing, location, transportation, and available resources.

Who owns the dog during temporary care?

Second Leash is designed around temporary care and reunification. The owner generally retains ownership, subject to signed agreements and applicable law.
